**How the sources fit together:** Newman supplies definitions, derivations and assumptions; the lecture selects the course’s sequence; the notebooks implement experiments. Student notes are a secondary record of a previous offering. When notation differs, use [[Notation and conventions]]. In particular, Newman’s Poisson degree-corrected SBM and the lecture’s Bernoulli SBM have different likelihoods. Small-world rewiring variants also differ; the textbook discusses this distinction.

Student Week 9 concerns network reconstruction; its inference assumptions should be checked separately, especially the graph-dependent Ising normaliser flagged in [[Corrections and caveats]].

Later student-only topics: Chapter 14 for communities, Chapter 15 (PDF 582) for percolation, Chapter 16 (PDF 620) for epidemics, Chapter 17 (PDF 688) for dynamical systems, and Chapter 18 (PDF 723) for search. These are contextual reading pointers, not confirmation of the 2026 syllabus after Week 7. See [[Student notes index]].
