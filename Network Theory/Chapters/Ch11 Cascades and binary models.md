## Key results at a glance
> [!summary]+ Memorise
> - Rates $F_{z,m}$, $R_{z,m}$; $\rho(t)$, $\rho_\infty$
> - Synchronous vs asynchronous ($\Delta t'=\Delta t/N$)
> - Threshold: adopt if $m_i/z_i>q_i$; vulnerable if $1/z_i>q_i$
> - Cascade if $\sum z^2\rho_zP(z)/\sum z\rho_zP(z)>2$
> - Infection probability $1-(1-\lambda)^m$; SIS mean field $\lambda\langle z\rangle>\mu$
